# The Grader panel

The **Grader** panel opens in the side bar on the right. In Cursor, click its icon instead (a page with a check mark and an arrow). The panel has five sections:

- **Account**: log in and out.
- **Current problem**: the problem of the file you are editing, your counted score, the language and **Submit to Grader**.
- **Result**: the verdict of every test after you submit.
- **History**: all your submissions for this problem, also the ones from the website.
- **Problems**: every problem with your counted score. Filter, search and click one to open its file.

You can drag the panel to the other side of the window if you prefer.
