# Submit

Press **Submit to Grader** (or the ☁ button in the editor title bar) and confirm.

- **Result** shows the status while grading, then one tile per test, the compiler message if compilation failed, and whether your counted score improved.
- The **counted score** is your best submission, not your latest, with hint / AI-assist penalties taken off, as on the website. Your instructor's final grading may differ.
- **History** lists every submission. Open an old version or compare it with your current file.

If the problem looks like part of an exam, the extension warns you first. Ask your instructor before using it in an exam.
